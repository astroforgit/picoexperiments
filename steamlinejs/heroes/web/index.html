Heroes of Lowrez

A tiny turn-based hex battle built for a future 8-bit port.

Mission

Defeat every red unit. Each hero may move and then attack, or skip at any time.

Controls

Mouse: select a hero, then a destination or enemy.

Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Enter confirms, Space skips, 1/2 changes battle, and R restarts.
