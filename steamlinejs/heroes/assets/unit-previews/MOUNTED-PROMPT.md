# Mounted unit atlas

Created with built-in imagegen. `medieval-mounted.png` supplies Scout, Ranger, and Cavalry in that row order. Each row has four walking/trotting frames, attack preparation, and attack release. The full horse and rider are preserved when converted into native 32×28 sprites.

## Prompt

Create a NEW sprite animation atlas matching detailed richly shaded 1990s Heroes of Might and Magic II medieval fantasy pixel art, realistic small full-body figures, not flat vector or chibi. True transparent alpha background. Exact grid SIX columns THREE rows. No labels or lines. Each row one mounted soldier ON A HORSE, entire rider, horse legs, tail and weapon visible, consistent scale, facing right. Row1 Scout: lightly equipped tan leather rider with short sword, unarmored chestnut horse, teal scarf. Row2 Ranger: green hooded leather rider carrying a spear upright, gray horse with green saddle blanket. Row3 Cavalry: silver plate armored knight with crimson plume, lance and shield, black horse with ornate crimson gold barding. Six poses per row: columns1-4 strictly WALK/TROT with different horse leg positions and rider bob, weapon always resting carried upright, no attack or thrust. Column5 attack preparation with weapon drawn back. Column6 attack release with sword slash / spear thrust / lance thrust. Match classic detailed medieval sprite shading and natural proportions with visible full horse and rider. Wide equal cells with generous transparent gutters, feet baseline consistent across all six frames. 1536x768 canvas desired. Do not draw checkerboard; real transparency.
