Greenhaven editor balance v2 — 17 October 2026

Open unit-editor.html directly or through the existing static server.
The editor is self-contained, including animated artwork and encounter rules.
The balanced defaults also live in greenhaven-unit-balance.json.

The preset adjusts all 12 player tiers and 13 enemy types. It retains individual
fighters, duplicate recruits, fixed damage and the current native turn rules.
Casters have ordinary ranged attacks; no unimplemented spells are assumed.
Start remains 50 gold, two Spearmen, level-2 Barracks and three army slots.
Chapel costs are 40 / 50 / 30. Slot upgrades plus three extra Spearmen cost
165 gold, matching all gold available before Forest stage 3 (50 + 50 + 65).
The third Chapel upgrade discounts recruits by 10 gold, breaking even after
three recruits receiving the full discount.

Encounters: four regions, three stages per region, three weighted groups per
stage except the single authored Warlock group. Each group has 1-5 enemies;
weight 0 disables a group. Add/remove groups, edit all enemy selections, stage
names, victory gold and region replay gold. Enemy identities are stable IDs.
A seed deterministically selects a group within its region/stage. Replays use
stage 3 and the smaller replay reward. Retry retains the seed in the intended
game integration; this editor only previews the encounter, not a campaign.
Terrain and initial deployment match the current native board.

The economy summary updates while editing. Strength estimates are heuristics,
not combat simulations or proven win rates. Warnings flag groups differing by
more than 30% estimated strength. These are initial playtest values.

JSON exports include encounterConfig version 1 alongside schemaVersion 2 unit
data. CSV includes settings and encounter variants. Legacy unit-only imports
receive the old fixed encounter tables and normalized derived range fields.
The existing native importer applies unit/building data only. Random encounter
pools are editor-only until integrated into the native generator/runtime.
Native content.json, assembly and executable have not been changed.

Progression update:
Dark Forest -> Catacombs -> Monster Caves -> Necromancer Ruins.
Three successful encounters clear a region by default. The next region stays
locked until its predecessor is cleared. Victories needed are editable from
3 to 9 per region and exported in encounterConfig.progression. Three internal
difficulty stages are distributed across that region's required victories.
The campaign preview enforces locks, counts test victories, grants each reward
once, and preserves the same encounter after test defeat. Retreat gives no
credit. Cleared-region replays give reduced gold and no extra victory credit.
This is an explicit test-result preview, not playable tactical combat. It resets
when the configuration changes. The separate authoring preview can inspect any
region and stage, including locked ones.

Active default groups have strictly increasing estimated strength bands:
Dark Forest 12-25.5; Catacombs 26.5-37; Monster Caves 38-48.5;
Necromancer Ruins 49-59.5. These are heuristics, not guaranteed combat difficulty.
Later enemies and groups were revised to preserve this ordering. The editor
warns if custom groups overlap a previous region's strength band.

Recovered Lowrez reference: globals.lua defines named encounter prerequisites;
battle_selector.gui_script.lua checks SNAPSHOT.battles_done[battle.req], filters
by party strength and hides completed once-only missions. For example Crypt
unlocks Graveyard, followed by Merry Men, Ogre Camp and Warlock. Its rosters are
fixed; terrain and some AI targeting are random. The new editor adapts the
prerequisite idea to consecutive region victory requirements and random pools.
