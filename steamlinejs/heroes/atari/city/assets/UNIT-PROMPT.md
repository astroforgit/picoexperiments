# Unit portrait atlas

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. The original is `units-source.png`;
the replaceable exported cells are `unit-<building>-<level>.png` (32×28 RGBA).

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game asset: ONE sprite atlas of fifteen colorful fantasy UNIT PORTRAITS for a retro Atari strategy game. EXACTLY five columns and three rows, all cells identical square size, perfectly regular 5 by 3 grid, no drawn borders or dividing lines. Solid pure magenta #FF00FF background between portraits for chroma key. Each cell contains a centered bust portrait (head, shoulders, recognizable weapon), generous clear magenta margin on ALL sides. Crisp clean chunky pixel art, strong outlines, large readable faces and silhouettes, limited saturated colors, readable when reduced to 32x28 pixels, facing viewer or slight three-quarter. Match cheerful medieval fantasy town style. Columns left to right: 1 red-and-blue human soldier with sword/shield; 2 green-clad human archer with bow; 3 teal woodland scout/ranger with hood; 4 white-and-gold cleric/healer with staff; 5 purple-and-cyan wizard with crystal staff. Rows show advancement: row1 humble militia, hunter, scout, acolyte, apprentice; row2 armored guard, archer, ranger, healer, mage; row3 majestic knight with steel helmet, marksman with fancy feathered hat, beastmaster with antler hood, priest with golden halo-like headdress, grand wizard with elaborate purple hat. Human fantasy adults, varied faces, heroic welcoming expressions. No letters, no text, no labels, no environment, no UI, no watermark. No sprite can touch another cell. All portraits use the same framing scale. 5:3 landscape atlas.
