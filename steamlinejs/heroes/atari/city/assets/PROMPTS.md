# Fantasy city artwork

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, not the CLI/API fallback.
Source PNGs are retained here. Individual building cells and the 320×200
background are build-time exports of those sources.

## City background prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production game asset: colorful fantasy medieval city BACKGROUND for Atari VBXE strategy game, landscape 8:5 aspect ratio, designed to remain readable at 320x200. Beautiful crisp retro pixel art, rich emerald grass, warm cobblestone paths, turquoise river along right edge, distant lavender mountains, bright blue sky, wooded city perimeter, golden afternoon sunlight. Fixed elevated three-quarter view. Five EMPTY grassy building plots clearly spaced in two rows: three across upper middle and two across lower middle. Paths connect them. No buildings on these five plots, leave room for tall upgrade buildings. Small stone city wall and gate at far background only. Upper 15 percent sky scenery, bottom 15 percent relatively quiet dark grass for later HUD. No text, no interface, no labels, no characters, no watermark. Broad pixel clusters and limited coordinated colors, no blur. This will be background under separately replaceable building sprites.

## Building atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single production sprite atlas for a colorful fantasy city building game. Produce EXACTLY a 5-column by 3-row evenly spaced sprite sheet, fifteen sprites total, all on a solid pure magenta #FF00FF background for chroma key extraction. Each cell equal size, no grid lines. Pixel art, fixed elevated three-quarter view, crisp chunky clusters, vivid warm fantasy colors, golden sunlight from upper left, dark outlines, suitable to reduce each cell to 56x64 pixels. Every sprite wholly inside its own cell with a generous magenta border, centered horizontally and grounded at the same bottom baseline in each cell. Columns: 1 military barracks with red tiled roof and blue pennant, 2 archery range timber cottage with green roof and a round target, 3 ranger lodge with teal roof and antlers, 4 chapel cream stone golden roof stained glass, 5 wizard tower purple roof cyan crystal. Rows are upgrades: row 1 small ONE-FLOOR humble buildings; row 2 same buildings with TWO FLOORS and richer details; row 3 same buildings with THREE FLOORS or majestic tower additions, distinctly taller and more advanced. Keep footprints similar and row1 buildings shorter within their cell, not rescaled to fill it. Strong individual silhouettes readable at tiny sizes. No scenery, no terrain bases, no text, no labels, no people, no shadows outside sprites, no watermark. Magenta background must remain flat and perfectly empty between all sprites. Landscape atlas aspect ratio 5:3. Original fantasy art.
