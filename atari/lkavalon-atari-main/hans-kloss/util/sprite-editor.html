Title export format

  • JSON stores every one of the 128 × 128 ANTIC mode 4 pixels, displayed at the correct double-width aspect ratio.
  • It also contains a 256-byte screen map and two 1 KB Atari font banks when the image uses no more than 128 unique 4 × 16 tiles.
  • Colors 3 and 4 share one bitmap value and are selected per tile, matching the Atari screen-code high bit.
  • If the tile limit is exceeded, the pixels are still exported and the editor reports how many tiles must be simplified.
  • The image autosaves separately from the piss animation.
