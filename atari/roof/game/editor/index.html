Night sky

Stars stay at fixed random positions. Clouds drift in front of them.

0% disables meteors; 100% sends one every ten seconds. Twinkling changes brightness gently without moving stars. Star counts update the scene preview; Build Atari applies all settings to the game.

Star fishing

Mostly still water. Smaller bites can lead to a rare one-second dive. The lower part of every float stays below the invisible sky surface.

Full casting power takes 0.5 seconds. More power throws farther forward and higher; every cast goes away from the fisherman.

Weights select which encounter follows a quiet interval. Catch and bait-loss chances are independent settings. Ignored dives lose the bait; no stars return until you reel in and rebait.

Occasional effects

Random mode plays one selected sound at a time, then waits for a random silence within your limits. Every selected sound has an equal chance. It uses the cleaner 46-level Atari playback. Sounds are stored in spare VBXE memory and copied into a RAM playback buffer during each pause. Use Start and Length (or drag on the waveform) to choose how much of each recording plays, until both meters fit.
