About this browser reconstruction

Built from the supplied Kult XEX: original enemy graphics, eight stage wave sets, 384 wave records and the translated enemy motion routine. This space variant adds a new Diabolic spaceship and layered parallax stars, nebulae and a distant planet. Building terrain and terrain collision are removed. This is a playable JavaScript adaptation, not an Atari emulator. Timing, collision, pickups, guardian behaviour, palette and synthesized sound effects are approximations. The original POKEY music and menus are not reproduced.

The game runs locally with no external downloads or accounts. Your best score is saved in this browser. Read the implementation notes ↗
