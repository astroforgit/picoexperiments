FOUR-FRAME BOSS RESOURCES

Sub-boss appearance

Four model-designed candidates. The end-of-level boss is the Insect Queen. Each uses four native 64×64 monochrome frames and the original guardian tile renderer. This changes sub-boss appearance across all sectors; attacks and health stay as configured below. Apply to local game, then press 1 in Atari.

Boss bullet patterns

Six original-sine-table patterns form slowly, then rush at four times ship speed. The opening alternates between upper and lower positions. New waves form while older guards finish passing. Ordinary enemy path edits do not change these attacks. Bosses fire straight or sine-wave orbs, retreat for four curtain waves or a meteor shower, then return. Shooting Off disables attacks.
