What matches Atari?

Atari speeds, 50 Hz updates, narrow player collisions, momentum, water drag, checkpoints, lava, cannons, moving bricks and offscreen thwomp attacks.

This is a browser simulation, not a hardware emulator. It runs smoothly even where real Atari hardware may drop frames. Sprite clipping and some subpixel edge cases can differ.
