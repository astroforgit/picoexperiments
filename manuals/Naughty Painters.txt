A local multiplayer competitive game.
Paint the most area of the floor with the color you are assigned to win.
Catch bombs to paint large area at a time.

Controls
Player 1:

    left/right : rotate, change arena in the start screen
    left+right : brake
    up/down : change a match time in the start screen

Player 2:

    s/f : rotate
    s+f : brake
