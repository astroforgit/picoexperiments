# Qubic Chess

Qubic Chess combines chess with a Rubik’s-style cube. Each of its six faces is an 8×8 chessboard. A player twists one slice, makes one chess move on the next face, and hands the turn to the opponent.

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser to play. The game runs offline; keep ambientssace.mp3 beside index.html for music. Black defaults to the computer. Choose **Player 2** before the first twist for local two-player play.

## How to play

1. Swipe a slice to preview a quarter-turn. Drag that same slice again or use the **90°** buttons to keep turning it. Four quarter-turns in one direction restore its starting position.
2. Drag elsewhere to orbit the cube, or select a board in the bottom panel to face it. The panel shows all six boards throughout play. During preview, compare **Before twist** and **After twist**. Gold marks the squares moved by the twist.
3. Select **Back** to restore the starting position and choose a different slice. Select **Accept** to commit the twist and go to the next face.
4. Select one of your pieces and a highlighted destination to complete the chess move.
5. After the computer plays, use **Previous** and **Next** to review its starting position, cube twist, and chess move. The bottom boards highlight the changes. Select **OK · Your turn** when ready. OK restores the computer’s final position even when you are viewing an earlier review step.

The active face uses a dark checkerboard with white borders. Each face has a slowly pulsing zodiac emblem that stays fixed while the pieces move. The selected slice turns translucent, and the cube’s surfaces briefly become translucent as gravitational waves pass through the scene.

## Audio and online play

Play online at https://astroforgit.github.io/picoexperiments/rubikchess/.

Open **Options** at the top to switch **Music** and **Sound effects** independently. Preferences are remembered in this browser. The ambient soundtrack loops and starts after your first click, tap, or keypress. Synthesized effects accompany slice twists, chess moves and captures, Back, Accept, computer review steps, and the return to your turn.
